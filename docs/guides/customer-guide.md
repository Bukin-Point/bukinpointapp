# Customer Guide

## Getting Started

Welcome to BukinPoint! This guide will help you book services and manage your appointments.

## Creating an Account

1. Visit the customer signup page
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm Password
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Any previous guest bookings with your email will be automatically linked

## Making a Booking

### Step 1: Find Provider
1. Get the provider's booking link
2. Or search for providers (if search is available)

### Step 2: Select Service
1. View available services
2. Click on a service to see details
3. Click "Select" to proceed

### Step 3: Choose Date & Time
1. Select a date from the calendar
2. View available time slots
3. Click on a time slot to proceed

### Step 4: Enter Information
1. If logged in, your details are pre-filled
2. If guest, enter:
   - Full Name
   - Phone Number
   - Email (optional)
   - Additional Notes (optional)
3. Review booking summary
4. Click "Complete Booking"

### Step 5: Confirmation
1. View booking confirmation
2. Note your booking reference
3. You'll receive confirmation details

## Viewing Your Bookings

### Dashboard
1. Navigate to your dashboard
2. View:
   - Booking statistics
   - Upcoming bookings
   - Past bookings

### Booking History
1. Navigate to "Bookings"
2. View all your bookings
3. Filter by status if needed

## Managing Your Profile

1. Navigate to "Profile"
2. View your account information
3. Update details as needed

## Tips

- Create an account for faster checkout
- Book in advance for popular time slots
- Keep your contact information up to date
- Check booking status regularly

## Support

For issues or questions, contact the provider or support.
