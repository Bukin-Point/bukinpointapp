# Staff Guide

## Getting Started

This guide will help you get started as a staff member on BukinPoint.

## Accepting an Invitation

1. Check your email for invitation from your provider
2. Click the "Accept Invitation" link
3. You'll be taken to the signup page
4. Fill in your details:
   - Name
   - Email (pre-filled)
   - Password
   - Confirm Password
5. Click "Create Account"
6. You'll be redirected to the dashboard

## Understanding Your Role

### OWNER Role
- Full access like provider
- Can manage staff, services, and all bookings
- Can access wallet and settings

### STAFF Role
- Limited access
- Can view services (read-only)
- Can view and manage own bookings only
- Can manage own availability
- Cannot access wallet, settings, or staff management

## Managing Your Availability

1. Navigate to "Availability" in the sidebar
2. You'll see your availability schedule
3. Click "Add Availability" to add time slots
4. Select day of week, start time, and end time
5. Click "Save"

**Note**: OWNER role can manage all staff availability. STAFF role can only manage their own.

## Viewing Your Bookings

1. Navigate to "Bookings" in the sidebar
2. You'll see bookings assigned to you
3. Filter by status if needed
4. Update booking status as needed

**Note**: OWNER role sees all provider bookings. STAFF role sees only their own.

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- Total bookings (your bookings only for STAFF role)
- Upcoming appointments
- Recent bookings

**Note**: Revenue and other sensitive metrics are hidden for STAFF role.

## Tips

- Keep your availability up to date
- Confirm bookings promptly
- Mark bookings as completed after service
- Contact provider for questions about permissions

## Support

For issues or questions, contact your provider or support.
