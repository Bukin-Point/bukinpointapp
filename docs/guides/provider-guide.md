# Provider Guide

## Getting Started

Welcome to BukinPoint! This guide will help you set up and manage your business on the platform.

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit the signup page
2. Enter your name, email, and password
3. Click "Sign Up"
4. You'll be redirected to onboarding

## Step 2: Complete Onboarding

1. Fill in your business details:
   - Business Name
   - Industry (searchable dropdown)
   - Phone Number
   - Business Email
   - Address (optional)
   - Timezone
2. Click "Create Profile"
3. Your wallet will be automatically initialized

## Step 3: Add Services

1. Navigate to "Services" in the sidebar
2. Click "Add Service"
3. Fill in service details:
   - Service Name
   - Description (optional)
   - Duration (minutes)
   - Price (₦)
   - Active Status
4. Click "Create Service"

## Step 4: Invite Staff (Optional)

1. Navigate to "Staff" in the sidebar
2. Click "Add Staff Member"
3. Enter staff email and select role:
   - **OWNER**: Full access like you
   - **STAFF**: Limited access (own bookings, availability)
4. Click "Send Invitation"
5. Staff will receive email with signup link

## Step 5: Set Availability

1. Navigate to "Availability" in the sidebar
2. Select staff member (or yourself)
3. Click "Add Availability"
4. Select day of week, start time, and end time
5. Click "Save"

## Managing Bookings

### View Bookings
- Navigate to "Bookings" to see all bookings
- Filter by status (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, etc.)

### Update Booking Status
1. Find the booking in the list
2. Click status dropdown
3. Select new status:
   - **Confirm**: Mark as confirmed
   - **Complete**: Mark as completed (triggers payment)
   - **Cancel**: Cancel booking
   - **No Show**: Mark as no-show

## Viewing Earnings

1. Navigate to "Wallet" in the sidebar
2. View:
   - Current balance
   - Total earnings
   - Transaction history

## Managing Settings

1. Navigate to "Settings" → "Business"
2. Update business details
3. Click "Save Changes"

## Tips

- Keep services active/inactive based on availability
- Set staff availability to control booking slots
- Monitor bookings regularly to confirm appointments
- Complete bookings to process payments
